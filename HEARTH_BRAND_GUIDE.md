## 2. Who we are

Hearth exists to bring warmth, comfort and nourishment back into busy homes. Chef Jordan shops for, cooks and packages wholesome, home-cooked meals right in her clients' kitchens, and leaves everything labeled and ready, so families can sit down to food made with care.

Hearth is more than meal prep. It's a lifestyle service: a reset for the kitchen, a rhythm for the week, and a reminder that how we treat our food is how we treat ourselves.

### Mission
To nourish families with food prepared with love and intention, and to teach people how to prepare food and show love to it.

### Philosophy
- **Food carries energy.** Jordan believes the way food is grown, handled, prepared and even spoken to changes what it gives back to the body. Even the frequency of an apple changes if you speak kindly to it.
- **Balance.** Yin and yang, dark and light: meals built for balance, not restriction.
- **Love made visible.** Every meal is cooked as if for her own family.
- **The kitchen is the heart of the home.** "Hearth" means the home's fire, the gathering place. The flame in the logo is that warmth.

*How to use the philosophy:* lead with warmth and practicality; let the deeper philosophy show on the My Story page and in small touches. Never preachy.

### What makes Hearth different
- **In your home, in your containers.** Not a delivery box from a commercial kitchen.
- **Cuisine from around the world.** Jordan has studied many cultures and cuisines and tailors every menu to the theme or request.
- **Personal.** Menus built around each family's tastes, dietary needs and favorites, and Jordan keeps a detailed profile for every household.
- **Concierge-level care.** Shopping, cooking, packaging, labeling and reheating instructions, all handled.
- **Wellness built in.** Small-batch wellness shots, specialty tea blends and personalized beverages.
- **Intention.** Food prepared with love and positive energy, not just efficiency.

## 3. Who we serve

**Primary:** women ages **35–65** who want warmth and comfort back in their kitchen and need help balancing grocery shopping, meal prep and packaging. Often busy professionals, mothers, caregivers for aging parents, or empty-nesters.

**Also:** high-net-worth individuals and families, discerning clients who value a high-touch service, adult children arranging care for an aging parent, and hosts planning special events or holidays.

**What they want to feel:** cared for, relieved, proud of what's on their table, and like someone who loves them cooked dinner.

**Common needs we meet:** dietary requirements (for example low-sodium, soft and easy-to-eat food, gluten-free, no added sugar), small portions for one or two, family-size meals, and holiday help.

## 4. What we sell

### Kitchen Reset (one-time welcome service)
A complete energy revamp of the client's kitchen before regular service begins: clearing, organizing, refreshing pantry and fridge, and setting the kitchen up for nourishing meals. Includes the client's **own set of glass meal containers and wellness-shot bottles**, sized to the household (they keep them). About 2–5 hours depending on the home. **Website price: "Inquire."**

### Weekly & Biweekly In-Home Meal Preparation
Jordan shops, cooks and packages meals in the client's kitchen on a confirmed day (arrival time flexible around grocery pickup). Every container is labeled with reheating instructions. Menus are customized for dietary needs and favorites; a monthly menu is shared ahead so clients can customize. Groceries are billed at cost, separately from the service. **Website price: "Inquire."**

### Personal Chef for Events
Intimate dinners, gatherings, celebrations and holiday meals with custom menus (Banquet Event Order for each event). **Website price: "Inquire."**

### Seasonal Holiday Menus
Order ahead, pay ahead, delivered. **Thanksgiving 2026 is the first** (menu and prices in the website brief, §6). Future: Christmas, Easter and other seasonal menus.

### Wellness Shots & Beverages
Small-batch 2 oz wellness shots, made fresh (examples: beet, pomegranate and ginger; carrot, orange, lemon and ginger; beet juice, pomegranate, apple cider vinegar, honey and cinnamon), plus specialty tea blends and personalized drinks. Offered as add-ons with regular service.

## 5. Food style

**Global cuisine, tailored to you.** Jordan has studied many cultures and cuisines, and Hearth cooks food from all over the world. Every menu is built around the client's request, a theme, an occasion, or a family's heritage and favorites.

- **World flavors:** Italian, Mediterranean, French, Middle Eastern, Latin American, Asian and Southern American comfort cooking, and more, cooked authentically and with respect for where each dish comes from.
- **Themed menus:** an Italian Sunday supper, a Mediterranean mezze table, a Moroccan-spiced dinner, a holiday feast; Jordan designs the menu to match the theme or request.
- **Comfort at the core:** whatever the cuisine, the food should feel warm, generous and home-cooked.
- **Seasonal and wholesome:** fresh produce, from-scratch sauces, breads and desserts.
- **Adaptable:** low-sodium, soft-textured, small portions, gluten-free and no-added-sugar versions as needed.
- **Presentation:** glass containers, neat labels, color and freshness visible through the glass; for events, abundant shared platters (the "table after the meal" look from the vision board).

Examples Jordan has cooked (for photos and copy): chicken piccata, braised short rib over soft polenta, chicken cacciatore, lemon chicken pastina soup, maple-glazed pork chop with sweet potato and pecans, pumpkin bisque, chocolate olive oil cake, beet-pomegranate wellness shots, a Thanksgiving herb-roasted turkey breast and pies, and a light charcuterie spread for events.

**On the website, say it simply:** "Cuisine from around the world, tailored to your table."

## 6. Voice & tone

**Sound like:** a warm, capable friend who happens to be a chef. Motherly, never fussy.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Speak in first person as Jordan ("I'll take care of the rest") | Sound corporate ("Our team leverages…") |
| Short, simple sentences | Long paragraphs or jargon |
| Describe food simply and appetizingly | Overwrite ("an exquisite symphony of flavors") |
| Words: warm, nourishing, home-cooked, cared for, gather, table, love, comfort, intention, fresh | Words: cheap, fast food, diet, cleanse, guilt-free |
| Sign off "With love, Jordan" | Use the word "circle" for community offerings |
| Mention reheating instructions and labeling (they reassure) | Show per-meal math, costs or margins |

**Sample lines**
- "Let me bring the warmth back to your kitchen."
- "Cuisine from around the world, tailored to your table."
- "Dinner's done. It's waiting in your fridge, labeled and ready."
- "Pick what you need, pay ahead, and put your feet up."
- "It should feel like a warm hug from mom waiting in your fridge."

### Type
- **Headings:** a warm classic serif (currently **DM Serif Display**; the logo serif is similar).
- **Body:** a clean, friendly sans-serif (currently **Figtree**).
- **Accent:** a flowing script, used sparingly, for "Chef Jordan," signatures and "With love."

### Graphic elements
- **Wax seals** (a flame or "H" seal) for badges, confirmations and packaging. Strong theme on Jordan's vision board.
- **Botanical line drawings** (herbs, olive branches, fruit) in cream on olive or sage.
- Circular emblem badges; subtle embossed/paper textures.
- Four-color band at the top of documents (red-brown, orange, gold, olive), used on menus and invoices.
- Seasonal illustrations (pumpkins, vines, leaves) for holiday pages.
- Rounded cards, soft shadows, generous white space.

### Photography
- **Signature shot: the table after the meal, from above.** Shared plates, crumbs, napkins. In Jordan's words, "the messiness is calming."
- **Sustainability:** glass "milkman" containers, reusable, zero waste. Show the glass containers proudly.
- **Jordan's face is central.** She is the brand. Professional portrait available: smiling, white chef coat with the embroidered Hearth logo, warm blurred kitchen behind her.
- Natural, warm light; real kitchens; hands at work; steam; glass containers of colorful food; ruby wellness shots; family tables.
- Feel: cozy, honest, abundant. Avoid sterile stock photos and over-styled flat-lays.
