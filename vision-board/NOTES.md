# Vision Board Notes — Hearth Kitchen Co.

**Source:** Jordan's Pinterest board, *Hearth Kitchen Co.* — https://www.pinterest.com/jordanrowlan/hearth-kitchen-co/ (61 pins; the 25 below were visible without signing in).

**Jordan's board description (her own words — treat as the design brief):**
> "Photographs of the table after a meal from above. The messiness is calming. Old slower ways of living using the glass container 'milk man' method. Zero waste sustainability."

> Images are other creators' work: **link to them for reference only; do not copy them into the site or this repo.**

## What the board says about Hearth

### 1. Palette: earthy, natural, old-world
The board is consistently **earth tones** — no plum or purple appears on it.

| Name (from pin 13 and repeats across the board) | Approx. hex (sampled) |
|---|---|
| Burnt Sienna / terracotta | `#914117` |
| Ivory Sand | `#E3D5C6` |
| Stormy Sky (soft blue-gray) | `#7D898F` |
| Sage Green | `#9A9F8C` |
| Deep olive | `#4F5223` |
| Dark moss / forest | `#534E3B` |
| Charcoal | `#282C30` |
| Kraft / warm tan | `#9E7A65` |
| Accent: antique gold foil | (use sparingly) |

**Decision (Nov 2026):** the logo and primary brand color move from plum to **terracotta / Burnt Sienna (`#914117`)** for now, so the whole brand sits inside this earthy palette.

### 2. Textures & materials
Linen, deckle-edge handmade paper, kraft paper, twine and ribbon, raw wood, stone, limewash/plaster walls, copper, matte ceramics, glass jars and containers.

### 3. Signature motifs
- **Wax seals** (on envelopes, cookie tops, kraft parcels) — a strong, repeated idea. Great for a site badge, order confirmations and packaging.
- **Botanical line drawings** — herbs, fruit, olive branches, leaves; white or cream line art on olive or sage.
- **Circular emblem / badge logos** with serif and script lettering, sometimes gold foil on dark green or charcoal.
- **Embossing / blind debossing** on cream paper — quiet luxury.

### 4. Photography mood
- **Overhead "table after the meal" shots** — shared plates, crumbs, napkins. Jordan finds the messiness calming: use this as a signature image style.
- Natural window light, soft shadows, hands at work with fresh herbs.
- Old-world European and farmhouse kitchens: open shelving, wood beams, arched stove alcoves, potted herbs.
- Sustainability: glass "milkman" containers, reusable, zero waste.

### 5. Typography
Classic serif headings + a delicate script accent; small, widely spaced capitals for labels. Simple and timeless, nothing trendy.

### 6. How to apply it on the website
- Backgrounds in Ivory Sand; text in charcoal or dark moss; buttons in Burnt Sienna; panels in Sage; footer in deep olive with cream text.
- A wax-seal style badge (flame or "H") for order confirmations and section dividers.
- Botanical line illustrations as light section accents instead of stock icons.
- Photo-led layout: Jordan's portrait, overhead table shots, glass containers of food, herbs in light.
- Paper-like texture (subtle grain) on backgrounds, never heavy.
- Thanksgiving: kraft-paper and wax-seal feel fits perfectly.
