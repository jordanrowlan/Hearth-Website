Policies

Clear terms so we can both focus on the food. Questions? Text or call 918-968-5002.

Deposits and payment

  • Events, dinners and gatherings: a 50% deposit is due at booking to reserve your date. The remaining balance is due in full on the day of your event.
  • Weekly and biweekly meal preparation: invoiced after each cook day, due upon receipt. Groceries are billed separately at cost, with receipts.
  • Pre-orders (like Thanksgiving): paid in full to confirm your order.
  • Pay online by card through your emailed invoice, or by check or cash made out to Hearth Kitchen Co., LLC.
  • Oklahoma sales tax is added to your invoice where it applies.

Cancellations and changes

Your deposit reserves a date on my calendar that I turn other clients away for, and it covers menu planning. For that reason:

  • 30 or more days before your event: your deposit can be moved one time to a new date within 6 months.
  • 8 to 29 days before: the deposit is non-refundable.
  • 7 days or less before: the deposit is non-refundable, plus the cost of any groceries and supplies already purchased for your event.
  • Within 72 hours: the full event total is due, since your food has been purchased and prep has begun.
  • Guest count: please confirm your final guest count 7 days before your event. I'm happy to add guests after that when I can, but the total won't go down.

Weekly and biweekly meal preparation

  • Please give at least 48 hours' notice to skip or move a cook day. Cook days changed with less notice are billed in full.
  • Groceries already purchased are billed at cost, with receipts available on request.

Pre-orders and delivery

  • Delivery area: delivery is available only within the Tulsa Metro area, about 20 miles from Broken Arrow. Orders outside the area will be refunded.
  • Changes and cancellations are accepted until the order deadline for a full refund. After the deadline, orders are non-refundable because ingredients are purchased for you.

If I have to cancel

If I ever need to cancel because of illness, emergency or severe weather, you'll choose between a new date or a full refund of everything you've paid.

Allergies

Please tell me about any food allergies when you book. My kitchen also handles wheat, dairy, eggs, tree nuts and other common allergens, so I can't promise a dish is completely allergen-free, but I will always do my best to keep you safe.

Privacy

When you send an inquiry or order, I receive your name, contact details, address and any notes you include, such as allergies. I use them only to plan your food, deliver it, and send your invoice. Form messages are delivered to me through Formspree, and payments are processed securely by Stripe, so I never see or store your full card number. I don't sell or share your information. To have your information deleted, email jordansrowlan@gmail.com.
